About India e-Visa Portal

Official digitized travel authorization platform for global citizens.

Simplified Travel Authorization

The India e-Visa Portal is the official digital gateway managed by the Bureau of Immigration under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. This system allows foreign nationals to submit their e-Visa applications entirely online, reducing paperwork and processing times.

Our platform handles travel authorizations for tourism, short business excursions, medical treatment, and academic conferences. With our secure digital structure, we ensure smooth and transparent visa handling directly to your email inbox.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant resolutions to standard queries regarding application review and guidelines.

Who is eligible to apply for an India e-Visa? ➕

Foreign nationals of over 160 countries who hold a passport with at least six months validity from the date of arrival in India. The purpose of visit must be tourism, short business, medical treatment, or brief conferences.

What is the standard processing time for an e-Visa? ➕

Standard processing time is 72 hours (3 business days) after application fee payment. We recommend applying at least 4 business days before your flight.

What documents do I need to upload? ➕

You will need a clean PDF scan of your passport's bio-data page (including photo and details) and a high-resolution, square digital photograph with a plain white background (JPEG format).

Is the e-Tourist Visa multi-entry? ➕

Yes, the 1-year and 5-year e-Tourist and e-Business visas allow multiple entries. The 30-day e-Tourist Visa allows double-entry.
